Frozen and Burst Pipes in Caldwell, ID

Protecting pipes before the next Arctic front

Frozen pipe repair in Caldwell is for the faucet that only trickles in a cold snap and the pipe that has split. Pick up the phone and call __PHONE_DISPLAY__, because water in a split pipe keeps flowing once it thaws.

What you may notice

  • A faucet that gives only a trickle, or nothing, in cold weather
  • Frost on an exposed pipe
  • A bulge, split or crack in a visible pipe
  • Water stains on a wall or ceiling after a thaw
  • Banging or hissing when the water comes back on

What is often behind it

  • Pipes in unheated spaces such as a garage, crawl space, attic or exterior wall
  • A garden hose left attached to an outdoor hose bib (the outdoor faucet)
  • Gaps in insulation where cold air reaches a pipe
  • Heat turned down too far in a vacant house or a closed-off room
  • Sprinkler or irrigation supply lines that were not drained before winter

Checks you can make yourself

Stick to checks that feel easy and safe. Never open a water heater, relight a pilot or touch gas piping or wiring; call instead.

  • Before freezing weather, disconnect, drain and store garden hoses. The Red Cross says to close the inside valve that feeds an outdoor hose bib and leave the outside bib open so leftover water can expand.
  • On very cold nights, open cabinet doors on exterior walls so warm room air reaches the pipes, and keep garage doors closed if supply lines run through the garage.
  • During a hard freeze, let cold water drip from a faucet served by exposed pipes. The Red Cross says even a trickle helps.
  • If you travel in cold weather, keep the heat on and set no lower than 55 degrees F, as the Red Cross advises.

Stop and phone if

  • A pipe has split or water is flowing from a joint: close the main shutoff and call
  • A faucet gives only a trickle and you cannot find or reach the frozen section
  • Water is near outlets, a light fixture or the electrical panel
  • A ceiling is bulging or a wall is wet after a thaw

Questions about frozen and burst pipes

How urgent is a frozen pipe?
Begin with a quick 1-to-10 gut check. Up at 8–10, like a split pipe, water near outlets or a bulging ceiling, close the main shutoff and call __PHONE_DISPLAY__ right away. In the middle (4–7), like a trickle from one faucet or frost on an exposed pipe, call within the week or before the next hard freeze. Down at 1–3, a cold night ahead, drain hoses and open cabinet doors on outside walls.
What winter lows should pipes be ready for?
The National Weather Service says winters are generally mild and below-zero lows are rare. Boise Airport recorded one sub-zero low from 1991 through 2008, and its record low is minus 25 degrees F from December 1990. The same office says the west end of the valley can run several degrees colder than Boise.
What do frost, freeze and hard freeze mean?
NWS Boise puts frost at 36 degrees F, a freeze at 32 and a hard freeze at 28. Exposed pipes and outdoor faucets are the first things to protect when a hard freeze is forecast.
Can I thaw a frozen pipe myself?
The Red Cross lists heating pads, hair dryers and towels soaked in hot water, and warns against blowtorches and any open flame. If you cannot find or reach the frozen section, or cannot thaw it, call a licensed plumber and look up the license with DOPL.
