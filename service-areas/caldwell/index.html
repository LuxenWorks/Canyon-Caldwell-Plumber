Plumber in Caldwell, ID: check these before any plumbing job

Plumbing in Caldwell means a city permit office, older lines on some streets and newer pipe on others. When a pipe leaks, start with leak repair. Call __PHONE_DISPLAY__, since one visit can show if it is a simple repair or a bigger job.

  1. How old the homes are

    For Caldwell city, Census ACS 2020-2024 5-year data (tables B25035 and B25034) show 22,378 housing units and a median build year of 2003, give or take 2 years. Of those, 6,524 (29 percent) were built in 1979 or earlier, including 1,426 from 1939 or earlier. The largest groups are 5,599 homes built from 2010 to 2019 and 5,448 from 2000 to 2009. In ZIP code tabulation area 83605, the Census Bureau's stand-in for the ZIP code, the median build year is 1987, give or take 5 years, and 6,569 of 14,391 units, about 46 percent, date from 1979 or earlier. A home built in 1979 is now at least 47 years old, old enough that original supply lines, drains and shutoff valves may be the source of leaks.

  2. Valley weather

    The National Weather Service Boise office describes Treasure Valley winters as generally mild, with rain more common than snow. Below-zero lows are rare: from 1991 through 2008 one occurred at Boise Airport, and the record low there is minus 25 degrees F on December 22, 1990. The same summary says an Arctic front, snow cover and a lasting inversion (a layer of warmer air sitting above cold air near the ground) can keep lows below zero for days, and that a persistent inversion with snow cover keeps winter temperatures at the west end of the valley several degrees colder than Boise. The summary does not name Caldwell, so treat Boise's numbers as a guide and protect exposed pipes and outdoor faucets before the first hard freeze, which NWS sets at 28 degrees F.

  3. Who issues the permit

    Inside the city, plumbing permits come from the City of Caldwell Building Safety Division, 205 South 6th Avenue, Caldwell, ID 83605, phone (208) 455-3024, with an inspection line at (208) 455-4605. Applications go through the city's online permit portal, paper submittals are no longer accepted, and the city asks applicants to allow 24 hours for processing. The city's residential packet says plumbing, electrical and mechanical work need separate permits and lists the Idaho State and Uniform Plumbing Code among the codes it applies. The city's pages do not say whether a water heater swap needs a permit, so ask before the work starts. Outside any city that runs its own program, Idaho Code 54-2620 sends the permit to the state: DOPL, 208-334-3950.

Before you call anyone in Caldwell

  • Find the main shutoff valve and make sure every adult in the house can reach it. Caldwell's water department lists an after-hours emergency line at (208) 250-1638.

  • Ask the Building Safety Division whether your job needs a plumbing permit before you sign anything, and ask for the permit number once it is issued.

  • Call the water department at (208) 455-3070 about hardness. The city's annual reports list wells and test results but no hardness figure.

  • Phone 811 ahead of any digging for a sewer or water line, including digging a contractor will do.

  • When an older Caldwell home shows a new stain or brown water, call __PHONE_DISPLAY__.

Questions about plumbing in Caldwell

  • Who issues plumbing permits in Caldwell?

    Inside the city, the Building Safety Division at 205 South 6th Avenue issues them; the phone is (208) 455-3024. Outside a city that runs its own program, the permit comes from the state at DOPL, 208-334-3950.

  • Where does Caldwell's water come from, and is it hard?

    The city's 2025 water quality report says supply comes from 15 groundwater wells across four pressure zones, with two storage tanks that hold 2.5 million gallons together. The report lists arsenic, nitrate and other test results but no hardness figure. Ask the water department at (208) 455-3070.

  • Who can call from Caldwell?

    Homeowners and landlords with a plumbing problem in Caldwell can call. Renting? Tell your landlord or building manager about it first. Get your address, the trouble and the age of the home ready, then call __PHONE_DISPLAY__.
