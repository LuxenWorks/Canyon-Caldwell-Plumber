Water Heater Repair in Caldwell, ID

Scale, leaks and permits for Canyon County heaters

What you may notice

  • Hot water that runs out sooner than it used to
  • Water that stays lukewarm or turns cold in the middle of a shower
  • Rusty or cloudy hot water
  • Popping or rumbling noises from the tank while it heats
  • Moisture or a puddle around the base of the heater

What is often behind it

  • Sediment and mineral scale settling on the tank bottom or coating a heating element
  • A failed heating element or thermostat in an electric heater
  • A gas burner or thermocouple (the safety sensor that proves the pilot flame is lit) that no longer works
  • A corroded tank, which cannot be repaired once it leaks
  • A broken dip tube, the pipe that sends incoming cold water to the bottom of the tank

Checks you can make yourself

Stick to checks that feel easy and safe. Never open a water heater, relight a pilot or touch gas piping or wiring; call instead.

  • Look at the floor, the tank and the pipes above it for moisture or rust streaks. Do not remove any cover or panel.
  • Read the rating label on the side of the tank and write down the brand, model and size to give the technician.
  • Run hot water at two different taps. If only one runs cool, the trouble may be that faucet and not the heater.
  • Note how long the hot water lasts, any noises, and when the change began.

Stop and phone if

  • You smell gas or rotten eggs near the heater: get everyone outside, then phone the gas utility from there
  • Water is pouring from the tank, the relief valve or the pipes above it: close the cold supply valve to the heater if you can reach it safely
  • You notice soot, a scorched smell or a yellow or orange flame at a gas appliance
  • Steam or boiling-hot water comes from a faucet or from the relief valve

Idaho and city details

  • Idaho Code 54-2604 lists water heaters, with their dedicated venting, as part of a plumbing system. The state's homeowner plumbing permit worksheet, revised in 2022, has a line for "Water Heater Replacement (only)" at $65.

  • Middleton's plumbing permit fees, effective October 1, 2026, list water heaters at a $65 basic fee per item. Caldwell's and Nampa's permit pages do not name water heaters, so confirm with their building offices before the work starts.

  • Nampa reports that its water ranges from no hardness up to 7.08 grains per gallon. The USGS says calcium carbonate scale can form when hard water is heated, which can shorten equipment life and raise heating costs.

Questions about water heater repair and replacement

How urgent is my water heater problem?
Begin with a quick 1-to-10 gut check. Up at 8–10, with a gas smell, water pouring from the tank or steam from a faucet, get everyone outside for gas and call 911 or the gas company from the yard. For water, close the cold supply valve if safe, then call __PHONE_DISPLAY__. In the middle (4–7), like rumbling, rusty water or a damp base, reach out within the week, since a small leak keeps growing. Down at 1–3, a slightly short supply of hot water, flushing sediment may help.
Is a permit needed to swap out a water heater in Caldwell, Nampa or Middleton?
Plan on it, and confirm with the office. Idaho law counts water heaters as plumbing, and Middleton lists them on its plumbing permit fees. Caldwell's and Nampa's pages do not mention water heaters, so ask the building office before the job starts.
Who is responsible for the permit?
Idaho issues plumbing permits to holders of a state certificate of competency or the firms they represent. Homeowners may buy a permit through the state for work on their own home. Get in writing the name of whoever buys the permit and whoever books the inspection.
Does hard water shorten a water heater's life?
The USGS says scale from heated hard water can reduce equipment life and raise heating costs. Nampa publishes a hardness range, while Caldwell's reports list none, so ask your utility or test your own tap.
