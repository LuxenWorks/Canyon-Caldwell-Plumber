    Begin with a quick 1-to-10 gut check. Up at 8–10, like water pouring out or sewage coming up, shut off the main valve if you can reach it safely and call (208) 837-7254 right away.   • How do I check a plumber's license in Idaho?

    Use the license search linked from the Idaho Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses State Plumbing Board page, dopl.idaho.gov/plb. Plumbing classes are contractor, journeyman and apprentice. For questions, DOPL's general line is 208-334-3233, weekdays 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.

  • Who issues plumbing permits in Caldwell, Nampa and Middleton?

    Each city issues its own: the Caldwell Building Safety Division at (208) 455-3024, the Nampa Building Department at (208) 468-5435, and the Middleton Building Department at (208) 585-3133. Outside a city that runs its own program, Idaho Code 54-2620 sends the permit to the state at DOPL.

  • Is a permit needed to swap out a water heater?

    Plan on it. Idaho law counts water heaters as part of the plumbing system, the state homeowner permit worksheet has a water heater replacement line, and Middleton lists water heaters on its plumbing permit fees. Confirm with the building office before the work starts.

  • Can I do plumbing work on my own home?

    Idaho Code 54-2602 excepts owners of single-family or duplex homes from the certificate requirement, though the work must still follow the state standards. Homeowners may buy a state permit for their own residence, and Nampa allows owners who will live in the home for 180 days after the work. Inspections still apply.

  • Do Caldwell, Nampa and Middleton publish water hardness?

    Nampa does: its 2025 report gives a range of none to 7.08 grains per gallon. Caldwell's 2025 report and Middleton's 2023 report list no hardness figure. Call the water department, or test a tap at home.

Three questions to settle before work starts

  1. Look up the Idaho license first

    Idaho requires a state certificate of competency to do plumbing for others. The State Plumbing Board lists contractor, journeyman and apprentice classes and a public license search. Ask for the contractor certificate number and verify it with DOPL before work starts; the general DOPL line is 208-334-3233.

  2. Find out who buys the permit and who books the inspection

    Caldwell's Building Safety Division, Nampa's Building Department and Middleton's Building Department each issue plumbing permits. Outside a city program, the permit comes from DOPL. Ask for the permit number in writing and the date of the inspection.

  3. Ask about the water, not only the pipe

    Hardness affects water heaters, faucets and softener choices, and the cities report it differently. Get your utility's range or test your own tap before you agree to equipment. Phone 811 ahead of any shovel work.
