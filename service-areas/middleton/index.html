  1. How old the homes are

    In Middleton city, Census ACS 2020-2024 5-year data (tables B25035 and B25034) count 3,707 housing units and a median build year of 2009, give or take 3 years. About 1,757 (47 percent) were built in 2010 or later and 2,623 (71 percent) in 2000 or later, while 450 (12 percent) date from 1979 or earlier. Small-city estimates carry wide margins of error, such as 1,293 homes built from 2010 to 2019 plus or minus 256, so treat the counts as approximate. The older 12 percent is the group most likely to have original supply lines and drains.

  2. Valley weather

    NWS Boise says strong winter inversions can last several days or even weeks when the weather pattern stalls, that winter fog is most likely from Thanksgiving to Valentine's Day, and that freezing rain and drizzle are rare at Boise but more common at the lower end of the Treasure Valley. The office puts a hard freeze at 28 degrees F, and exposed pipes and outdoor faucets are the ones to protect before one arrives.

  3. Who issues the permit

    Plumbing permits in Middleton come from the City of Middleton Building Department, 1103 West Main Street, Middleton, ID 83644, phone (208) 585-3133. The city's fee schedule, Resolution 548-26 effective October 1, 2026, includes a plumbing permit form: existing residential work costs $65 plus $10 per fixture, and lawn sprinklers, water heaters and water conditioners each carry a $65 basic fee per item. Permits and inspection requests run through the city's online permit portal. The city code lists "repairing, but not replacing, existing plumbing fixtures" among work exempt from a building permit.

Before you call anyone in Middleton

  • Ask the Building Department for the current plumbing permit form before work on a water heater or water conditioner, and ask who will request the inspection.

  • Read your utility bill closely. The fee schedule lists a base rate plus use charges, and sewer use is figured from the November-through-March monthly average.

  • The city's 2023 water quality report names seven wells and gives no hardness figure. Ask Public Works at (208) 585-3133 whether a newer report exists.

  • Save the Public Works after-hours number, (208) 921-0029, near the main shutoff for burst-pipe emergencies.

Questions about plumbing in Middleton

  • Who issues plumbing permits in Middleton?

    The City of Middleton Building Department, 1103 West Main Street, (208) 585-3133. Its fee schedule includes a plumbing permit, and applications and inspection requests go through the city's online portal.

  • Where does Middleton's water come from?

    The city's 2023 Consumer Confidence Report says Middleton draws drinking water from seven wells located throughout the city. It reports nitrate at 1 ppm against a limit of 10 ppm and gives no hardness figure.
