== Post: Before the first hard freeze: hoses and hose bibs ==
National Weather Service Boise puts a hard freeze at 28 degrees F. Before one arrives, disconnect, drain and store garden hoses. The Red Cross also says to close the inside valve that feeds each outdoor hose bib and leave the outside bib open, so water left in the pipe has room to expand. On very cold nights, open cabinet doors on exterior walls so room air reaches the pipes. Know where the main shutoff valve is before you need it. When a hose bib drips or a pipe splits in a freeze, call [PHONE], because water left running keeps damaging the house.

== Post: Nampa's water hardness runs from none to 7.08 grains ==
