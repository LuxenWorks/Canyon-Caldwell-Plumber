Drain Cleaning in Caldwell, ID

Slow drains and backups, from trap to sewer main

Drain cleaning in Caldwell is for the sink that drains slowly, the tub that gurgles and the floor drain that smells. Call __PHONE_DISPLAY__, because one blocked trap and a blocked main line need different fixes.

What you may notice

  • Water that drains slowly from a sink, tub or shower
  • Gurgling from a drain when another fixture is used
  • Sewage odor near a floor drain or under a sink
  • Water rising in a tub or floor drain when the washer empties
  • Several fixtures running slow at the same time

What is often behind it

  • Hair, soap film and grease collecting in a trap (the curved pipe under a fixture that holds a water seal against sewer odor)
  • Wipes, paper towels and hygiene products that do not break apart in the line
  • Roots entering a joint in an older buried line
  • A blocked vent pipe, the pipe that lets air into the drainage system so water can flow
  • A sag in a buried line where solids settle and pile up

Checks you can make yourself

Stick to checks that feel easy and safe. Never open a water heater, relight a pilot or touch gas piping or wiring; call instead.

  • Note which fixtures are slow. One slow fixture points to a local clog; several at once point toward the main line.
  • Lift out the stopper or hair catcher on a slow sink or tub and clean it by hand, wearing gloves.
  • Look at the lowest drain in the house, such as a basement or laundry floor drain, for standing water or odor.
  • Skip chemical drain cleaner on a drain that is fully blocked. The liquid can sit in the pipe and splash on whoever opens it next. Tell the technician if you already used some.

Stop and phone if

  • Sewage is coming up in a tub, shower or floor drain
  • Toilets bubble or back up when the washer or a sink drains
  • Wastewater is spreading across a floor near outlets, appliances or the electrical panel
  • A drain stays blocked after one careful try with a plunger

Questions about drain cleaning

How urgent is a clogged drain?
Use a quick 1-to-10 gut check. Up at 8–10, like sewage in a tub or floor drain or wastewater near outlets, stop using water and call __PHONE_DISPLAY__ right away. In the middle (4–7), like several slow drains or gurgles after the washer runs, reach out within the week, since a main clog keeps growing. Down at 1–3, one slow sink, try a plunger once and clear the trap.
What is the difference between a clogged drain and a clogged sewer line?
A clogged drain affects one fixture or one branch. A clogged sewer line affects every fixture, because all of them empty into it. Several slow fixtures, or sewage rising in the lowest drain, point to the main line.
Is a camera inspection needed every time?
Not for a single slow sink. A camera makes sense when the same drain clogs again, when several fixtures are slow, or when roots or a damaged section are suspected.
Does clearing a blocked drain need a permit in Idaho?
Clearing a blockage on its own does not, under Idaho Code 54-2621. A repair that changes the pipe, such as replacing a section of drain, can. Ask the building office in your town before work begins.
Does this cover Nampa and Middleton too?
Yes. Drain help covers Nampa and Middleton along with Caldwell. Call __PHONE_DISPLAY__ and give your address.
